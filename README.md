## What I do

I work on intelligent systems at the intersection of **robotics**, **embodied AI**, **embedded software**, and **cybersecurity**.

My focus is on building practical systems that connect perception, reasoning, simulation, and real-world deployment.

---

## Current focus

- Research Assistant at **Towson University**
- Building **Repair2Skill**, a vision-language-action framework for robotic furniture repair
- Developing embedded and robotics projects using **Python, C/C++, PyTorch, OpenCV, Raspberry Pi, CAN, and J1939**
- Exploring secure engineering practices across AI, embedded systems, and developer tooling
